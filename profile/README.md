# 🫀 Laboratory for Signal and Image Processing (LTSI)

Welcome to the official GitHub of **LTSI**, a multidisciplinary research lab at the University of Rennes and INSERM.  
Our research lies at the interface of **health**, **information technologies**, and **biomedical engineering**, with goals that are methodological, technological, and clinical.  
We focus on **interpretation and decision support**, leveraging the synergy of **signal, model, and image**.

---

## 🔍 Research Overview
LTSI conducts research through **five specialized teams**:

- **CINETYKS** – Dynamics of neuronal systems in epilepsy.
- **SEPIA** – Therapeutic stimulation and personalized monitoring for heart failure and apnea-bradycardia.
- **IMPACT** – Decision support via imaging and modeling for surgical and therapeutic planning (cardiovascular and radiotherapy).
- **MEDICIS** – Knowledge modeling for surgical and interventional decision-making.
- **DOMASIA** – Big Data and Learning Information Systems (patients → data → knowledge → practice).

Our work combines **signal processing**, **image analysis**, **sensor technologies**, and **modeling**, supported by transversal projects in engineering sciences.

---

## 🌍 International & Collaborative Projects
LTSI is actively involved in **European and international research programs**:
- **FP7**: ICT-FET HIVE, euHEART, VPH, MEDIATE
- **H2020**: LUMINOUS, EurValve, DigiNew-B, MEDIRAD
- **ERA PerMed**: PerPlanRT
- **ERC Synergy**: Galvani
- **Horizon Europe**: SMASH-HCM, PAROMA-MED

We are founders of **CominLabs** and **CAMI**, and co-creators of the **CRIBs Sino-French Biomedical Information Research Center**.

---

## 🛠 Platforms & Innovation
Our technological platforms include:
- **Cardiology Research Platform** (CHU Rennes, CIC-IT 804)
- **TherA-Image** for image-guided therapy and minimally invasive surgery
- **TherA-Tech** for preclinical research on implantable medical devices
